# Operational Briefing: Quiet Day Shift

Welcome to your daytime SOC shift. You are monitoring real-time enterprise security alerts across identity providers, secure email gateways, and endpoint detection agents.

### Objectives
1. **Queue Triage**: Evaluate all incoming alerts in your workspace queue.
2. **Sender Authentication**: Differentiate spoofed phishing links from verified corporate newsletters.
3. **Authentication Velocity**: Differentiate automated SSH scans from routine administrative logins.
4. **Shift Submission**: Group verified true positive alerts into an investigation case, escalate critical threats, and submit your shift evaluation.

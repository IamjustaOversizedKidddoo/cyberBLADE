# Scenario Practice: Operational Noise & False-Positive Dissection

Focuses entirely on building discipline against alert fatigue: handling scanner bursts, scheduled patch scripts, flapping legacy network triggers, and self-service password resets.

### Learning Goals
- Dissect benign true positives vs false positives.
- Recognize authorized administrative activity without reflexive escalation.
- Provide crisp, defensible closing notes for non-malicious operational telemetry.

# Operational Briefing: Night Shift with Telemetry Gaps

An overnight surveillance shift during low-activity hours. Scheduled maintenance on central network taps has created intermittent telemetry blind spots.

### Operational Protocols
- Rely on secondary log conduits (cloud management audit logs, flow records, proxy traces) when primary endpoint alerts are delayed.
- Audit off-hours administrator privilege changes and access key registrations against change management logs.
- Identify unauthorized mass document transfers to unencrypted removable media.
